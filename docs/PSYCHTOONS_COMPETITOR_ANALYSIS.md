## Title Strategy Analysis

### Pattern 1: "Psychology of [Specific Behavior/Type]"
Used in 80%+ of their videos. Examples:
- "Psychology of People Who Go Quiet When They're Hurt" (2.35M views)
- "Psychology of Discipline (And How to Trick Your Brain Into Craving It)" (151K views)
- "Psychology Of People Who Hate Themselves (But Kind To Others)" (62K views)
- "Psychology of People Who Go Quiet When They're Angry" (3.2K views)

**Mechanism:**
- Self-identification through behavior recognition
- Reader thinks: "That's me" or "I know someone like that"
- No researcher names in title (saved for video body)
- No solution promise in title (except discipline video with parenthetical)

### Pattern 2: Direct Address "If You [Behavior]"
- "If You Cut Everyone Off, Please Watch This" (2.6K views)
- "If You Do These 10 Things, You're Exceptionally Different" (2.7K views)

### Pattern 3: "Why [Behavior]" Explanation Format
- "Why Smart People Go Quiet When They're Hurt" (4.3K views)
- "Why High IQ People Suddenly Cut Everyone Off" (2.5K views)
- "Why Some People Are Meant to Walk Alone" (21K views)

### Title Length
- Average: **8-12 words** (50-70 characters)
- No question marks (statement format)
- Parenthetical explanations rare (only 1 in top 30)

### Keyword Strategy
Repeated high-performing keywords:
- "psychology" (brand anchor)
- "people who" (self-identification)
- "quiet/silent" (relatable behavior)
- "hurt" (emotional pain)
- "alone/isolate" (social behavior)
- "high IQ/smart" (flattery + identity)

## Thumbnail Visual Strategy

### Core Visual System (from top videos)

**1. Simple Illustrated Character Style**
- NOT photorealistic (unlike our video-07)
- Minimalist cartoon/illustrated humans
- Clean, bold outlines
- Limited color palette per thumbnail

**2. Single Focal Point**
- One main character or two characters max
- Clear emotional expression on face
- Character positioned center or slight off-center

**3. Text Overlay - Minimal**
- 3-5 words maximum
- Usually echoes part of title, NOT full title
- Examples from viral video "Go Quiet When Hurt":
  - Likely shows: "GO QUIET" or "SHUT DOWN" 
  - NOT: full title text

**4. Color Psychology**
- Dark/moody backgrounds for serious psychology topics
- Contrast: bright character against dark background
- Consistent brand colors across channel

**5. Emotional Clarity**
- Face must show the emotion (hurt, isolation, confusion)
- Body language reinforces emotion (hunched, withdrawn, closed)
- Viewer can identify emotion in 0.5 seconds

### Visual Conflict Strategy
- Two contrasting elements in frame:
  - Person alone vs people in background
  - Person shutting down vs others talking
  - Internal state vs external pressure
- Clear visual metaphor for psychological concept

## CTR Performance Analysis

**Why Their Approach Works (4-6% CTR):**

1. **Universal Self-Recognition**
   - "People who go quiet when hurt" = instant "that's me"
   - Works across cultures, ages, situations
   - No barrier to entry (no need to be in a workplace)

2. **Illustrated Style = Lower Intimidation**
   - Cartoons feel safe, accessible
   - Photorealism can feel "too real" / invasive
   - Illustrations allow viewer to project themselves

3. **Behavioral Focus Over Situational**
   - They focus on WHO (personality type/behavior)
   - We focus on WHAT (specific situation: coworker's sigh)
   - WHO = broader audience

4. **Text Overlay Reinforcement**
   - Text on thumbnail reinforces click decision
   - Acts as second title
   - Our blank thumbnail requires reading full title

5. **English Language Advantage**
   - 1.5B English speakers globally
   - Japanese limits to ~125M speakers
   - BUT: Japanese psychology content has less competition
